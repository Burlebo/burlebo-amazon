Knockout floods in from the image edges and clears only background-colored pixels connected to the border, then fills them pure white. Your subject is never read or altered — interior regions (like an arm) can't be reached. Raise Background match if some background is left behind; lower it if it starts nibbling the subject edge. Works best on a clean, fairly even studio background. AI mode handles cluttered backgrounds but, like any cutout model, can clip a low-contrast edge — use the per-image Touch up brush to fix the rare miss in either mode.
